Healings of facial pain (trigeminal neuralgia)
Healing of trigeminal neuralgia (pain affecting one side of the face)
Mr L. N. (32), Kerkrade (Netherlands)
In 1983, I began to have trigeminal neuralgia which manifested as daily attacks of pain lasting one to two hours. The pain extended across the right half of my head and affected my forehead, my eyes and my right upper jaw. A neurologist operated on me and burnt through a facial nerve. Subsequently my lower jaw was completely numb for three months, yet the pain in the right half of my face recurred on a daily basis. I declined a second operation and struggled on with the help of pain killers. Finally the pains became so unbearable that I contemplated suicide.
Six days after my introduction to the teachings of Bruno Gröning, in April 1990, I was completely free from pain. Since then I have been healed from these terrible nerve pains.
Healing of trigeminal neuralgia (pain affecting one side of the face)
Mrs R. K. (55), Meschede (Germany)
After a serious car accident, I received neurological therapy for 14 years to combat trigeminal nerve pain and numbness on the right side of my face. At first I obtained relief from the pain for one or two weeks through Tegretol, Rökan (Ginkgo biloba), vitamin tablets and infusions but in the course of time, the attacks of pain grew progressively worse until they were almost unbearable. The therapy no longer helped me. In this condition I came to the Bruno Gröning Circle of Friends.
After my introduction, in August 1991, I absorbed the healing stream on a daily basis. Until the spring of 1992 I continued to have these almost unbearable attacks of pain. Then, from one day to the next, the pain stopped and the numbness disappeared. Since then I have had no recurrence of this problem.
