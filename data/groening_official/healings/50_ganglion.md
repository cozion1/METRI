Healings of ganglion
Healing of ganglion
Mr J. R. F. (37), Lüneburg (Germany)
For two years I suffered increasing pain day and night in my right hand caused by a ganglion, about one centimeter in circumference. This threatened to jeopardise my career as a musician and piano teacher. Neurotherapeutic and psychotherapeutic measures, as well as magnetic therapy and Reiki all only resulted in stopping the growth for a short while. An operation seemed unavoidable, but I refused it because I had heard that surgically-removed ganglions often grew back.
Three months after my introduction to the teachings of Bruno Gröning, in November 1992, I received absolute healing which has remained until today. The ganglion has completely vanished and I can play the piano again for hours without any problems.
Healing of ganglion
Mrs V. S. (40), Heiligenroth (Germany)
For about five years I suffered from a painful ganglion on my left thumb near my wrist. My doctor told me that it had to be operated, but I did not want this.
Some weeks after my introduction to the teachings of Bruno Gröning, in June 1988, and after I had ignored the ganglion for a while, I suddenly felt intense pain in my hand and a few hours later I saw that the ganglion had disappeared.
