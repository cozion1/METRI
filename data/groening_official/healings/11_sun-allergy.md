Healings of sun allergy
Healing of allergy against printer's ink and sunlight
Mrs G. M.-G. (47), Bochum (Germany)
I was allergic to the sun for 12 years. My neck, shoulders and chest were particularly affected. Even after only a few minutes' exposure to the sun, wheals and swellings appeared, which were painful and itchy. Even a special, very expensive sun protector was of little help. I just had to ensure that my clothing covered me completely.
In 1993 I was introduced to the teachings of Bruno Gröning because I was seeking help for my husband. A year later I went to Sicily on holiday. On the first evening there I suddenly realised that I had no allergic reactions, even though I had been all day in the sun without any special protection. This healing has persisted until today and I am wholeheartedly thankful for it.
Healing of allergy against printer's ink and sunlight
Mrs M. B. (37), Frankfurt (Germany)
Every time I returned to my homeland, Mauritius, I had an allergic reaction to the sun. This manifested as redness and oozing pustules. Sun lotion with a high protection factor and medically prescribed ointments did not help. This condition only improved after 14 days when my skin had become accustomed to the sun.
A year after my introduction to the teachings of Bruno Gröning I went to Mauritius. Although the temperature was 40 degrees Centigrade I had no reaction. Three years later it was the same. I have been healed since joining the community.
