Healings of nose pain (nasociliary neuralgia)
Healing of pain around the eyes and the nose
Mrs G. F. (39), Koblenz (Germany)
In May 1991 my doctor diagnosed neuralgia of the nervus nasocilians. My nose ran continuously and I suffered stabbing attacks of pain in it. My eyes ran, I was hot and my general condition left a lot to be desired. When these attacks were particularly severe, I went to my doctor four and five times a day because I could not stand the pain. Medicines like Novalgin, Tramal and Xylocain (Lidocaine) gave me a few hours of relief. On average my doctor had to visit me twice a week, at night, to inject painkillers.
In August 1991 I absorbed the healing power for the first time. Directly afterwards I was free from pain. Since then the problems have never recurred. The neuralgia has simply disappeared.
