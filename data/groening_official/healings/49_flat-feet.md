Healings of flat feet
Healing of talipes transversoplanus (flatfoot, splayfoot)
Mr R. L. (35), Nüremberg (Germany)
As a child I had splayfeet. They often pained me. I was given arch supports but too much standing or walking still gave me pain.
This was the situation when I was introduced to the teachings of Bruno Gröning in the year 1988. Since October 1989 I no longer need arch support and since December of the same year my feet have been pain-free, even after walking for several hours.
Healing of talipes transversoplanus (flatfoot, splayfoot)
Mrs E. L. (55), Cologne (Germany)
For 25 years I always had to wear arch supports because of my splayfeet. These helped my feet not to tire and ache so rapidly. I could only wear special shoes which could accommodate arch supports but never fashionable shoes.
Six weeks after my introduction to the teachings of Bruno Gröning, in the summer of 1989, I felt an intense burning sensation under my feet and increased circulation in my feet and legs. Four weeks after my introduction it occurred to me one morning that I no longer needed arch supports. From that day on I have worn fashionable shoes again and no longer have aching feet so that I can take long walks.
