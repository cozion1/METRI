Healings of disabilities after surgery
Healing of paralytic symptoms after inguinal hernia repair
Mrs L. T. (59), Meschede (Germany)
Twenty-one years ago I had a hernia operation. Afterwards my right leg tilted outwards when I sat or lay if I didn't support it, and caused a very uncomfortable pulling sensation from my groin to my stomach.
In spring 1988 I came to the Circle of Friends. The following July it struck me that my leg was completely in order again and it has remained so to date.
Healing of problems related to the intake and digestion of fat after a gallbladder operation
Mrs I. H. (63), Bebra (Germany)
In 1959 my gall bladder, which was full of stones, was removed. Subsequently I no longer had gall bladder colic. However, after eating foods which were not easily digestible like pulses, cabbage, fried food or food prepared with fat and onions, I always felt bloated and had pain in my upper stomach. The pain radiated to my back. Because of this, I had to maintain a diet.
Since my introduction to the teachings of Bruno Gröning, in 1986, I noticed that food did not affect me so much. About a year later, I was able to eat everything, including fatty foods, without any problems.
Healing of problems related to the intake and digestion of fat after a gallbladder operation
Mrs S. D. (64), Bielefeld (Germany)
Three years after a 1967 gall bladder operation, the same colicky pains I had previously experienced after eating certain foods, recurred. These were in my upper abdominal region and in addition I had headaches and nausea. At the time, and until 1981, I was living in Poland and often had to call in an emergency doctor. I only kept going by taking medicine (Enadin, later Oragallin-S) after every meal.
About two weeks after my introduction to the teachings of Bruno Gröning, in 1990, I was extremely hungry and allowed myself to be carried away by the urge to eat some warm apple cake which had just come out of the oven. Although I didn't take any medicine afterwards, I had no repercussions for the first time in 22 years. Since then I have been able to eat everything without medication.
Healing of skin graft (problem associated with graft)
Mrs A. F. (61), Eupen (Belgium)
In December 1987 I severely burnt the instep of my left foot with boiling cooking fat and was in great pain. In hospital, skin from my thigh was transplanted onto my foot. Subsequently I had weeks of treatment with ointments, bandages and medication and could only walk with a crutch. During the summer of 1988 my foot started to itch strongly so that I often scratched it until it bled. In time the skin started to shrink and was totally encrusted. Due to the continuous pain, I was advised to have another transplant.
But this never took place, because three months after my introduction to the teachings of Bruno Gröning, in September 1990, I received total healing. The skin on my left foot is supple once again, it does not itch and the pains have gone.
