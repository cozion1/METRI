Healing of tachyarrhythmia
Mrs A. K. (46), Stuttgart (Germany)
From the autumn of 1988 I used to wake up every night, often several times, panic-stricken and with a racing heart. It was one or two hours before I could fall asleep again. My doctor prescribed homeopathic tablets which did not help.
A week after my introduction to the teachings of Bruno Gröning, in January 1989, all these problems had ceased and I sleep undisturbed through the night.
Healing of chronic tachyarrhythmia
Mrs C. L. (56), Braunschweig (Germany)
In 1984 I contracted cardiac dysrhythmia which manifested as a sort of stumbling, double heartbeat and tachycardia. At first this occurred every 2-3 weeks, lasted about ten minutes and made me feel very anxious. My pulse rate was up to 180 a minute. I was prescribed the following medications: Metoprolol Tartrate and Verapamil HCI. They shortened the duration of my attacks, but in turn these now became more frequent. My house doctor said: "Nothing can be done. You must learn to live with this condition."
Since I began to absorb the healing stream in conjunction with the teachings of Bruno Gröning I have had no more problems.
