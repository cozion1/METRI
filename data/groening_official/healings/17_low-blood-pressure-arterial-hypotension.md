Healings of low blood pressure (arterial hypotension)
Healing of hypotension (low blood pressure) and circulatory problems
Mrs J. D. (39), Oberhausen (Germany)
For about 20 years I suffered from a lack of concentration. I had a dull feeling in my head and was often dizzy. My doctor ascribed this to my blood pressure, which at 70/40 was far too low. I took medicines, had acupuncture and went in for various sporting activities. Nothing helped, or brought only short-term improvement. One day I fainted in a department store and after that I was afraid to venture into crowded areas.
In the summer of 1995 I came across the Bruno Gröning Circle of Friends at an exhibition. I was introduced to his teachings. The following day all my problems were gone and I even forgot to keep taking my blood pressure tablets. At my next control examination my doctor established that my blood pressure was normal (110/90). I have had no further problems and my blood pressure has remained stable.
Healing of hypotension (low blood pressure) and circulatory problems
Mrs N. D. (49), Frankfurt (Germany)
For years I suffered from circulatory disturbances which manifested as numbness, particularly in my arms. At night especially I had pins and needles in my arms and feet, and they were ice cold. Because of this I kept waking up and in the morning I was tired and dazed.
After my introduction to the teachings of Bruno Gröning all my problems disappeared, including my numb, cold arms and feet, and once again I can sleep wonderfully well through the night.
Healing of hypotension (low blood pressure) and circulatory problems
Mrs B. K. (28), Nordenham (Germany)
For four years I suffered recurrently from low blood pressure (90/50) when it was hot and sultry, or when I worked in hothouses with high temperatures and humidity. Despite medication (Pholedrin-longo) to raise my blood pressure and relieve the resulting problems (nausea, dizziness, violent heart palpitations and tachycardia to the point of fainting) I was incapable of working during extended periods of hot weather.
Since my introduction to the teachings of Bruno Gröning and my regular absorption of the healing stream I have no more problems. My blood pressure is now around 135/90 and I am able to enjoy the summer. I no longer need medication.
