Healings of gout (arthritis urika)
Healing of gout
Mrs L. F. (67), Passau (Germany)
About six years ago, the basal joint of my right big toe became knotted with gout. It was continually inflamed and caused me severe stabbing pain. A change of diet and Allopurinol shortened the attacks somewhat, but the stabbing, throbbing pain continued, sometimes even when I was lying down.
In December 1994 I saw a television programme about the teachings of Bruno Gröning. I was introduced in January 1995 and from then onwards absorbed the healing stream. I felt warmth and tingling in my toes, and approximately three months later my foot was completely healed. There have been no further attacks of gout.
