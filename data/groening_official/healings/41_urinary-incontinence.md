החלמה מאי שליטה במתן שתן
מאריה וובל (בת 64), טרויכטלינגן (גרמניה)
סבלתי מחולשה בשלפוחית השתן מאז ניתוח בבטן התחתונה שעברתי בשנת 1980. זה התחיל כדלקת מתמשכת בשלפוחית השתן. במשך הזמן פיתחתי חולשה תמידית בשלפוחית השתן. חשתי לחץ חזק בשלפוחית השתן כך שנאלצתי לתת שתן כל 20-25 דקות ביום. בריצות מהירות, השתעלויות או קפיצות, השתן זרם ללא שליטה. לפעמים, הייתי צריכה להחליף תחתונים מספר פעמים ביום, אף על פי שהשתמשתי במגני תחתון.

החלמה מבריחת שתן (הטלת שתן בלתי נשלטת, לא רצונית)
גב' מ.ד. (בת 61), אינגולסטאדט (גרמניה)
מגיל 14 סבלתי משלפוחית שתן חלשה, היה עלי להחליף תחתונים שלוש פעמים ביום. תחבושות הגייניות וצמר גפן לא עזרו. הייתי בשירותים באופן תמידי. משום שהייתה לי זרימה מתמשכת של שתן, היה לי מאד לא נעים במיוחד כאשר ביקרתי אצל מישהו. זו הסיבה לכך שהייתי אצל הרופא לעיתים קרובות. בשנת 1977, כרתו לי את הרחם ובאותו זמן ניסו לתקן לי את הבעיה בשלפוחית השתן. לשווא. בנובמבר 1990, הרופא שלי המליץ לי על ניתוח נוסף.
ביולי 1991, התוודעתי לשיטה של ברונו גרונינג. שלושה חודשים לאחר מכן, לא הייתה לי יותר בעיה בשלפוחית השתן. מאז לא הופיע שום סימן של חולשתי לשעבר.
Healing of urinary incontinence (uncontrollable, involuntary urination)
Mrs G. W. (63), Wiesbaden (Germany)
My bladder problems began in 1987 with pressure on my bladder and some stomach pains. When I went to the toilet only a few drops came away but as I left the house I could not control my bladder. I always wore a pad and eventually had to take a spare one with me. During the night I had to go to the toilet as often as four times. A medical examination showed that I had second degree urinary stress incontinence.
When I was introduced to the teachings of Bruno Gröning, in November 1990, I could not at first believe that I too could be helped. But a few days later I was aware of a distinct improvement, and after another week all my problems were gone. Since then, my pads have been dry and I have been able to sleep through the night.
Healing of urinary incontinence (uncontrollable, involuntary urination)
Mrs H. St. (49), Olpe (Germany)
For fifteen years I suffered from incontinence and always had to wear panty liners. When I did sport I even had to wear proper incontinence pads. According to my doctor, the cause was a prolapse of the bladder and uterus and he recommended an operation.
One month later I was introduced to the teachings of Bruno Gröning. After three months my condition worsened, but 14 days later it suddenly vanished. My doctor confirmed that an operation was no longer necessary.
Healing of urinary incontinence (uncontrollable, involuntary urination)
Mrs A. C. (74), Luttich (Belgium)
I suffered from incontinence for about eight years. When I walked I lost urine on a drop by drop basis at every step so I always had to wear protective pads which had to be changed several times a day. At rest or at night the problem did not occur. Remedial gymnastics achieved a temporary and minimal improvement.
In the year following my introduction to the teachings of Bruno Gröning, in August 1993, I noticed no change. During the following six months the problem steadily decreased and disappeared totally in February 1995. Now, even a strong sneeze does not result in a loss of urine.
Healing of urinary incontinence (uncontrollable, involuntary urination)
Mrs H. R. (57), Hannover (Germany)
After an operation on my lower abdomen in 1981 I developed a bladder problem. I could no longer control my urine and had to wear pads. The toilet always had to be nearby because I had to pass water very often. At night I had to get up four or five times. My doctor did not advise an operation and said that I would have to live with the condition. I was not given any medication.
This situation continued until my introduction to the teachings of Bruno Gröning, in October 1994. In the months following my introduction I still had to go often to the toilet but after about six months I could control the condition during the day and only got up once during the night. Now I can go everywhere quite confidently without searching continually for a toilet.
