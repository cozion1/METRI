Healings of disabilities due to frostbite
Healing of pain in the legs after frostbite
Mrs E. K. (62), Cologne (Germany)
As a refugee in the winter of 1945, I contracted frostbite in both legs. From then onwards I suffered severe pain in them, both day and night. These pains stretched from my toes to my hips. Both legs were red, raw and swollen from my ankles to my knees. I could no longer walk properly because the legs were weak, as if they had no life left in them. The ointments prescribed by my doctor had little effect.
After the introductory talk to the teachings of Bruno Gröning at the end of April 1991, I was free from pain and this has remained so to date. My legs feel light and are no longer raw. I can walk again and sleep through the night without pain.
Healing of pain in the hands after frostbite
Mr K. H. (31), Schmallenberg (Germany)
I contracted frostbite in both hands during my military service and suffered the consequences for seven years. When it was cold I had stabbing pains in the back of my hands up to my wrists, and they prevented me from working properly.
During the winter following my introduction to the teachings of Bruno Gröning, my thumbs swelled so severely for two days that I couldn't continue my work as a blacksmith. A few months later, while on a two-hour bicycle ride in below freezing temperature, I realised that I had forgotten my gloves. The dreaded pains did not recur. I have remained free from pain until today.
